# Client Simulation

## A. Session Identification
<!-- kind:identification -->

## B. Learning Outcomes
<!-- kind:outcomes -->
By the end of this session, the learner will be able to:
1. Receive and interpret a client brief professionally.
2. Plan a design approach based on the brief.
3. Create initial concepts that meet the brief requirements.
4. Present work to a "client" (instructor) and receive feedback.
5. Handle feedback professionally and make revisions.
6. Deliver final files in the correct format.
7. Reflect on the process and identify lessons learned.

## D. OPENING ACTIVITY (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:opening -->
"The Simulation Begins"
Instructor Script: "Good morning, everyone. Today is different. Today, you are not students — you are professional designers.
I am your client. I have a design project for you, and I expect professional work.
You will receive a brief, create a design, present it to me, receive feedback, make revisions and deliver the final work. This is
exactly how real design projects work. Let us begin."

## E. PREREQUISITE REVIEW (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:prereq_review -->
Review Questions and Expected Answers:
1. "What are the six steps of the design process?"
Research, plan, create, review, revise, deliver.
2. "What are the six key questions to ask a client?"
Purpose, audience, information, format, timeline, budget.
3. "How many revision rounds should you include?"
2–3 rounds.
4. "What is the most important rule when receiving feedback?"
Listen carefully and do not get defensive.
5. "What should you deliver to a client?"
Final exported files, source files (if requested), and a README file (optional).

## F. FULL LECTURE NOTES (10 minutes)
<!-- kind:lecture -->
The Simulation Explained
Instructor Script: "Here is how the simulation works:
Phase 1: Receive the Brief (5 minutes) I will give you a design brief. Read it carefully. Ask me any clarifying questions.
Phase 2: Create the Design (30 minutes) Create a design that meets the brief. Apply all the principles you have learned. Save
multiple versions as you work.
Phase 3: Present to the Client (10 minutes) Present your design to me. Explain your decisions. I will give you feedback.
Phase 4: Revise (15 minutes) Make the changes I requested. Present the revised design.
Phase 5: Deliver (5 minutes) Export the final files and deliver them to me.
Phase 6: Reflect (5 minutes) Write a short reflection on what you learned from the process."

## I. INDEPENDENT CLASS EXERCISE (70 minutes)
<!-- kind:exercise -->
Exercise: "Client Simulation"
Scenario: "You are a professional designer. You have received a brief from a new client. Complete the project from brief to
delivery."
Simulated Client Brief: "I am opening a new restaurant in Port Harcourt called 'Taste of Lagos.' I need a flyer to promote my
grand opening. Here are the details:
Event: Grand Opening Celebration
Date: Saturday, 15th November 2026
Time: 10am – 10pm
Venue: 123 Aba Road, Port Harcourt
Special Offer: 20% off all meals on opening day
Contact: 0801 234 5678, tasteoflagos.ng
Target Audience: Local residents, food lovers, families
Tone: Warm, inviting, celebratory
Format: A4 flyer (print)
Brand Colours: Red and gold
Deadline: Today
Please create a flyer that captures the excitement of the grand opening and makes people want to come. I look forward to
seeing your work."
Task: Complete the following phases:
Phase 1: Receive the Brief (5 minutes)
Read the brief carefully.
Ask the "client" (instructor) any clarifying questions.
Phase 2: Create the Design (30 minutes)
Research similar restaurant flyers for inspiration.
Sketch a rough layout.
Create the design in Canva.
Apply all design principles.
Save multiple versions.
Phase 3: Present to the Client (10 minutes)
Present your design to the instructor.
Explain your decisions.
Receive feedback.
Phase 4: Revise (15 minutes)
Make the changes requested by the "client."
Present the revised design.
Phase 5: Deliver (5 minutes)
Export as PDF Print.
Save in Canva with a descriptive name.
Phase 6: Reflect (5 minutes)
Write a short reflection on what you learned.
Requirements:
Complete all six phases.
Apply all design principles.
Handle feedback professionally.
Deliver in the correct format.
Time Allowance: 70 minutes
Success Criteria:
Design meets all brief requirements.
All design principles applied.
Feedback handled professionally.
Final files delivered correctly.
Complete Answer Key for the Instructor:
There is no single correct answer. The instructor evaluates based on:

## J. REAL-WORLD CASE STUDY (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:case_study -->
Case: "The First Client"
Situation: Adaeze has just completed the Graphic Design course. A friend asks her to design a flyer for a small event. Adaeze is
nervous — this is her first real client. She remembers the simulation from the course and follows the same process.
The Problem: Adaeze is nervous because she has never worked with a real client before.
Relevant Concept: The professional workflow. Because Adaeze practised the workflow in the simulation, she knows exactly
what to do.
Worked Solution: Adaeze:
1. Asks her friend the six key questions.
2. Creates a design based on the brief.
3. Presents the design and explains her decisions.
4. Receives feedback and makes revisions.
5. Delivers the final files.
6. Her friend is thrilled and recommends Adaeze to others.
Transfer Exercise: "You have just finished the course and someone asks you to design a flyer. What is the first thing you do?"

## K. COMMON MISTAKES AND TROUBLESHOOTING (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:troubleshooting -->
Mistake 1: Not Asking Clarifying Questions
Symptoms: The designer starts work without fully understanding the brief. Fix: Always ask clarifying questions before starting.
Mistake 2: Presenting Without Explanation
Symptoms: The designer shows the design without explaining their decisions. Fix: Always explain your decisions — the client
needs to understand your reasoning.
Mistake 3: Arguing with Feedback
Symptoms: The designer disagrees with the client's feedback. Fix: Listen, ask questions, evaluate, then respond professionally.
Mistake 4: Not Saving Versions
Symptoms: The designer overwrites previous versions. Fix: Save each version with a version number.
Mistake 5: Missing the Deadline
Symptoms: The designer does not deliver on time. Fix: Plan your time carefully and communicate if you need more time.

## M. KNOWLEDGE CHECK (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:knowledge_check -->
1. What is the first thing you should do when you receive a client brief? Answer: Read it carefully and ask clarifying
questions.
2. What should you do before presenting a design to a client? Answer: Review the design against the brief and check that all
requirements are met.
3. How should you respond when a client gives you feedback you disagree with? Answer: Listen carefully, ask clarifying
questions, evaluate the feedback, then respond professionally.
4. What should you deliver to a client at the end of a project? Answer: Final exported files, source files (if requested), and a
README file (optional).
5. What is the most important skill in client work? Answer: Communication — listening, asking questions and explaining
decisions.

## O. Instructor Evaluation Notes
<!-- kind:instructor_notes -->
Observable Signs of Competence
The learner interprets the brief correctly.
The learner creates a design that meets the brief.
The learner presents confidently and explains decisions.
The learner handles feedback professionally.
The learner delivers final files correctly.
Common Misconceptions
"The client is always right." Teach: the client knows their business, but you are the design expert. Sometimes you need to
respectfully push back.
"Feedback means failure." Teach: feedback is a normal part of the process.
"Speed is more important than quality." Teach: deliver on time, but never sacrifice quality.
Differentiation Guidance
Level 1: Provide additional guidance during each phase.
Level 2: Complete the simulation as written.
Level 3: Act as the "client" for another learner.
