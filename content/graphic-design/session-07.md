## B. Learning Outcomes
<!-- kind:outcomes -->

1. Identify the key elements of a business flyer: headline, subheadline, body, call to action, contact information.
2. Apply design principles to create a professional flyer.
3. Identify the key elements of a social media post: visual, headline, call to action.
4. Design for Instagram and Facebook using correct dimensions.
5. Design WhatsApp graphics appropriately.
6. Use Canva's flyer and social media templates as starting points.
7. Apply brand consistency across multiple design formats.

## F. FULL LECTURE NOTES (35 minutes)
<!-- kind:lecture -->
Topic 1: Business Flyer Design (15 minutes)
Instructor Script: "A flyer is a single-page design that promotes a business, event or offer. It is one of the most common design
types in Nigeria. Every salon, restaurant, church, shop and event needs flyers.
Key Elements of a Business Flyer:
1. Business Name: The most important element. It should be the largest text on the flyer.
2. Tagline or Offer: A short phrase that tells the reader what the business offers or what the special offer is.
3. Key Information: What, where, when. The essential details.
4. Call to Action: What you want the reader to do — "Call Now," "Visit Us," "Order Today."
5. Contact Information: Phone number, address, website, social media handles.
6. Visual Element: An image or illustration that supports the message.
Flyer Design Principles:
1. Hierarchy: The business name is the biggest. The offer is second. The details are third. The contact information is smallest.
2. Contrast: Use dark text on a light background or light text on a dark background.
3. Alignment: Left-align all text for a clean, organised look.
4. Balance: Distribute visual weight evenly. Do not put all the text on one side and all the images on the other.
5. White Space: Leave breathing room around text and images.
Common Flyer Mistakes:
Too much text — the reader does not know what to focus on.
Poor contrast — text is hard to read.
No call to action — the reader does not know what to do.
Missing contact information — the reader cannot reach the business."
Topic 2: Social Media Graphic Design (15 minutes)
Instructor Script: "Social media graphics are designs created specifically for platforms like Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp.
They have different requirements from flyers.
Instagram Posts:
Dimensions: 1080 x 1080 pixels (square) or 1080 x 1350 pixels (portrait)
Design for mobile viewing — most people view Instagram on their phones.
Use bold, readable text — small text is hard to read on a phone.
Keep the design simple — you have less than 3 seconds to grab attention.
Facebook Posts:
Dimensions: 1200 x 628 pixels (landscape)
Design for both desktop and mobile viewing.
Facebook posts appear in a feed, so they need to stand out among other content.
WhatsApp Graphics:
Dimensions: 1080 x 1080 pixels (square) or similar
WhatsApp is used for more personal, informal communication.
Designs should feel friendly and direct.
Common uses: birthday wishes, event invitations, business promotions, price lists.
Social Media Design Principles:
1. Simplicity: Social media designs should be simple and immediate. If it takes more than 3 seconds to understand, it is too
complex.
2. Bold Text: Use large, bold text that is readable on a phone screen.
3. One Message: Each post should have one clear message. Do not try to say everything in one post.
4. Call to Action: Every post should tell the reader what to do — "Order Now," "Send a Message," "Click the Link."
5. Brand Consistency: Use your brand colours and fonts in every post.
Topic 3: Using Canva Templates Effectively (5 minutes)
Instructor Script: "Canva has thousands of flyer and social media templates. Templates are a great starting point, but you must
customise them to make them your own.
How to Use Templates Effectively:
1. Choose a template that matches your purpose. Do not choose a template just because it looks nice. Choose one that has
the right structure for your message.
2. Change the colours to your brand colours. Do not keep the template's default colours.
3. Change the fonts to your brand fonts. Do not keep the template's default fonts.
4. Replace the images with your own. Do not keep the template's stock images unless they are relevant.
5. Adjust the layout if needed. Move elements, resize text, delete unnecessary elements.
6. Add your brand elements. Add your logo, brand colours and contact information.
Template Mistakes:
Using a template without customising it — it looks generic.
Choosing a template that does not match the purpose — the structure is wrong.
Keeping too many template elements — the design looks cluttered."

## J. REAL-WORLD CASE STUDY (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:case_study -->
Case: "The Restaurant That Doubled Its Customers"
Situation: A restaurant in Port Harcourt has been struggling to attract customers. Their flyer is cluttered, with too many fonts
and colours. Their social media posts are inconsistent — different colours and fonts for each post. A designer is hired to
redesign their marketing materials.
The Problem: The restaurant's marketing materials look unprofessional and inconsistent. Customers do not recognise the
brand. The flyer does not communicate clearly. The social media posts do not stand out.
Relevant Concept: Flyer and social media design. The designer applied all the principles — hierarchy, contrast, alignment,
balance, white space and brand consistency.
Worked Solution: The designer:
1. Created a clean flyer with clear hierarchy — business name, tagline, key information, call to action.
2. Used the restaurant's brand colours consistently across all materials.
3. Created simple social media posts with one message and one call to action.
4. Within three months, customer visits double.
Transfer Exercise: "Your friend runs a small business and wants to promote it on social media. What are three things you would
tell her to make her posts more effective?"

## K. COMMON MISTAKES AND TROUBLESHOOTING (5 minutes)
<!-- kind:troubleshooting -->
Mistake 1: Too Much Text on a Flyer
Symptoms: The flyer is covered with text. Fix: Remove unnecessary information. Keep only the essentials — name, offer, key
details, call to action.
Mistake 2: No Call to Action
Symptoms: The flyer does not tell the reader what to do. Fix: Add a clear call to action — "Call Now," "Visit Us," "Order Today."
Mistake 3: Inconsistent Branding Across Formats
Symptoms: The flyer, Instagram post and Facebook post all look different. Fix: Use the same brand colours and fonts across all
designs.
Mistake 4: Complex Social Media Posts
Symptoms: The social media post tries to say too much. Fix: One message per post. If you have more to say, create multiple
posts.
Mistake 5: Poor Image Quality
Symptoms: The images are blurry or pixelated. Fix: Use high-quality images from Canva's photo library or your own photos.
